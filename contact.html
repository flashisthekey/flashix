Need Help?

Flashix provides customer assistance directly through our built-in Support Chat system. If you need help with wallet access, transactions, withdrawals, account guidance, or technical issues, simply open the support chat and connect with our team.

How to Contact Support

Step 1: Open Support Chat
Tap the floating chat icon available on the wallet pages to launch the live support window instantly.
Step 2: Enter Basic Details
Enter your name and optional email address so our team can identify your request and respond efficiently.
Step 3: Describe Your Issue
Explain your concern clearly such as withdrawal delay, balance issue, login trouble, wallet guidance, or any other request.
Step 4: Attach Screenshot (Optional)
You may upload screenshots using the image button to help support understand your issue faster.

Response Time

Most messages receive a quick reply depending on support volume. Our team aims to provide fast and helpful responses for all users.

Important Notice

Never share passwords, private keys, or sensitive recovery details in chat. Flashix support will never ask for confidential credentials.
